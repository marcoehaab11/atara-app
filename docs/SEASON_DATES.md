Product windows: Ramadan ends the day before Fitr; Fitr lasts three calendar
days, Adha four, and Sham El-Nessim one. These are celebration windows, not the
government's potentially longer employee holidays. Review `THEME_DATES` annually;
unlisted years use Normal automatically. No dates are silently extrapolated.
